GAMEPLAY:
You play as simple citizen in a civilization where you will basically live there. 
You will experience the kings' rulings , the economical state of the civilization , will witness the fall of civilization or participate on rising a new one. 
You can witness the construction of the great pyramids or even participate in the French and Indian war and so on. 
As the player proceeds deep enough in the events they will gain XP to level up and that's where our second subject begins...
