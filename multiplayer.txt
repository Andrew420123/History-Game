MULTIPLAYER:
Multiplayer will serve two sides: Educational and Gaming sides

Educational side:
Will evolve on players competing against each other on who solves the questions or quizzes faster and most importantly correctly. 
Playing their will reward the player with XP , cosmetics and weapons that can't be claimed by any means other than the mentioned method. 
There where also a global leaderboard to add some spice to the competition. Playing this side will be unlimited (No energy needed to participate).

GAMING side:
Unlike the educational side , energy is required to participate in this side. 
Here it will be a 1v1 duels featuring each two players' avatars as warriors and are using weapons claimed from leveling up.
The ONLY way to obtain energy to participate is only by testing your historical knowledge in the EDUCATIONAL side. 
There will also be a global leaderboard here too to add some heat. So basically it's just two noble warriors fighting till death claim ONE of their souls.
