LEVEL UP SYSTEM
By playing the game , gaining enough XP and testing the player's knowledge , They can level up. The benefits of leveling up are as following:
1- Unlocking more historical events to try
2- Unlocking cool cosmetics to design your avatar
3- Unlocking weapons to use in multiplayer or side quests 

But how do we level up? Simple by playing and completing side quests.
Side quests will play a huge role in the game as it's the only way to grind for XP (After playing he game ofc). 
Side quests mostly will evolve around answering some questions or taking quizzes to test one's historical knowledge.
Or it can be a boss fight or doings some certain actions in order to complete the side quest in question.
